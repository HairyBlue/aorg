# Personal Assistant, MPOR & Job Scout Crew (`assistant`)

An 11-agent personal assistant, operations, and career crew led by Concierge. Separates general daily/weekly progress and Trello/Kanban drafts from selected-row workplace MPOR/SPOR reporting, alongside priority planning, inbox triage, Second Brain notes, non-coding ops logging, and remote job scouting.

- **Team Head:** `concierge`
- **Bots (11):** `concierge`, `job-scout`, `job-scout-2`, `job-scout-3`, `job-scout-4`, `job-scout-5`, `mpor-reporter`, `daily-progress-reporter`, `inbox-triage`, `second-brain`, `admin-ops`
