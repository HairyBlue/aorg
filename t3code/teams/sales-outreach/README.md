# Sales, Lead Qualification & Outreach Crew (`sales-outreach`)

A 3-agent B2B lead generation and outreach crew led by Revenue Lead. Qualifies public leads and companies against your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), drafts specific non-spammy outreach messages, and prepares grounded follow-ups for stalled conversations without sending automatically.

- **Team Head:** `revenue-lead`
- **Bots (3):** `revenue-lead`, `lead-miner`, `outreach-closer`
