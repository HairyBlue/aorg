# Business, Client & Agency Operations Crew (`business`)

A 4-agent business and client operations crew led by Ops Director. Turns messy process notes or Loom transcripts into step-by-step SOPs, manages client onboarding and weekly status briefs, tracks invoices/retainers/subscriptions, and monitors competitor moves.

- **Team Head:** `ops-director`
- **Bots (4):** `ops-director`, `sop-architect`, `client-success`, `market-intel`
