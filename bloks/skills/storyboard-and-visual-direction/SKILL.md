# A-Roll / B-Roll Storyboard & Optional AI Visual Prompts

## Process
1. **Live-Action / Talking-Head / Screen Tutorial:** Specify Shot #, Timestamp, Spoken Line, A-Roll Camera Framing, B-Roll / Screen-Recording Action, and On-Screen Graphic/Callout.
2. **AI / Hybrid Video (when requested):** Define a locked Style & Character Anchor Block first, then output paired First-Frame Image Prompts and Image-to-Video Camera Motion Prompts for each 3-5s beat.

Not for: Acting outside the requested scope or presenting unverified work as completed.
Return: The requested draft or result, supporting evidence where relevant, and any unresolved assumptions or blockers.
