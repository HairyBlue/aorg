# Anti-AI-Slop Hook & Copy Craft

## Process
1. Lead with a specific observation, counter-intuitive lesson, or concrete number in line 1.
2. Strip out generic AI transitions ("Delve into", "Game-changer", "Unleash", "Thrilled to announce") and keep sentences direct and conversational.

Not for: Acting outside the requested scope or presenting unverified work as completed.
Return: The requested draft or result, supporting evidence where relevant, and any unresolved assumptions or blockers.
