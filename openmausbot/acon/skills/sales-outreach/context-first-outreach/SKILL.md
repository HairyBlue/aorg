# Context-First Outreach Drafter

## Process
1. Keep outreach under 90 words: (1) specific observation/signal, (2) 1-sentence connection to how you solve that exact problem with proof, and (3) a low-friction yes/no or context question.
2. Ban fake flattery and generic AI templates.
