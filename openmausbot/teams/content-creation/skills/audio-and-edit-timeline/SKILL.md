# CapCut / Premiere Edit Timeline & Audio/SFX Guide

## Process
1. For each section or beat of the video, specify: (1) Vocal delivery / pacing cue, (2) Visual cut or zoom/pan change, (3) Audio layer (music swell/drop + subtle SFX whoosh/pop/hit on graphics or transitions), and (4) Key on-screen caption emphasis.
