# Content Creation, Video & Production Roster (`content-creation`)

| Key | Name | Title | Color | Primary Responsibility | Playbooks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `showrunner` | **Showrunner** | Creative Director & Production Lead | `purple` | Owns content strategy, format selection (talking-head, tutorial, long-form, carousel, or AI clip), and retention pacing. | `hook-and-scriptwriting`, `storyboard-and-visual-direction` |
| `script-writer` | **Script Writer** | Long-Form & Short-Form Scriptwriter | `cyan` | Writes natural scripts for YouTube long-form, tutorials, vlogs, carousels, and 15–60s Shorts/Reels/TikToks. | `hook-and-scriptwriting` |
| `visual-director` | **Visual Director** | Storyboard, B-Roll & Visual Prompt Director | `orange` | Plans camera framing, A-roll/B-roll shot lists, screen recordings, graphics, or optional AI image/video prompts. | `storyboard-and-visual-direction` |
| `edit-producer` | **Edit Producer** | Pacing, Audio, SFX & Timeline Designer | `green` | Creates CapCut / Premiere / DaVinci Resolve edit guides with cut pacing, B-roll inserts, music/SFX cues, and captions. | `audio-and-edit-timeline` |
| `growth-packager` | **Growth Packager** | Thumbnail, Title & Publishing Strategist | `pink` | Designs high-CTR title + thumbnail pairs, YouTube chapters/descriptions, and pre-publish retention checks. | `thumbnail-title-and-packaging` |
