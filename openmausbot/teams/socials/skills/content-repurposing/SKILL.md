# 1-to-Many Content Repurposing

## Process
1. Extract the single sharpest insight, story, or proof point from the source material.
2. Produce: (1) 3 distinct hook options, (2) a punchy X post + 5-part thread, (3) a LinkedIn post formatted for skimmability, (4) an IG/FB caption or carousel slide outline, and (5) a 30-to-60-second vertical video script.
